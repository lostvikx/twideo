Good to know

Questions, answered.

Is Twideo free?

Yes. No account, no extension, and no ads. Twideo is a static page that reads public post metadata through the FxTwitter API.

Why does the video open instead of downloading?

The links point directly to video.twimg.com, X's video CDN. Browsers play MP4 files inline instead of opening a save dialog. Use the player's menu or right-click → Save video as… On mobile, use the share or save option.

Which resolutions are available?

Only the versions X exposes for that post, usually 270p through 4K. Twideo lists every MP4 variant, highest first. Vertical videos are labeled by their short side, with exact dimensions shown next to each option.

Does Twideo host or store videos?

No. Every file is served by twimg directly to your browser. Twideo has no backend and stores no media.

Does it work with private or age-restricted posts?

No. Only public posts with native videos or GIFs. Deleted, protected, or login-gated posts will fail.

What data does Twideo store?

Recent lookups — post ID, handle, and video count — stay in your browser's local storage so you can reopen them. There are no accounts, cookies, or analytics. The post ID is sent from your browser to api.fxtwitter.com to fetch metadata.

Is Twideo affiliated with X?

No. X and Twitter are trademarks of X Corp. Twideo is an independent tool. Respect copyright and X's terms, and only download content you have the right to save.
